zenbridge turned off "Stay on across restarts"

Older versions of zenbridge saved every setting when you changed any of them, so "Stay on across restarts" could stay switched on after its default became off. While Claude is on, any Claude session on this computer can use your browser, so this update switched it off once and turned Claude off.

To use Claude, click the zenbridge button on a new tab. It turns itself off after 8 hours, or when you press Alt+Shift+K. If you really want it to stay on across restarts, switch it back on in Settings.

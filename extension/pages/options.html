Local addresses

One per line, like http://localhost:* (:* means any port). A tab you hand Claude on one of these may use all of them; a tab on a public site never can, unless you Shift+click to add one.

zenbridge is an unofficial, community project. It is not made by or affiliated with Anthropic. Every action is logged on your computer only (macOS: ~/Library/Logs/zenbridge). Typed text is not logged.
